Orbit

Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Operated by Zac Gordon

Orbit is a private contacts app. It keeps your data on your own devices. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.

Google data Orbit reads

If you connect a Google account, Orbit asks for each Google service only when you turn it on, and only reads:

  • Gmail: message headers only — sender, recipients, subject and date. Never message bodies or attachments.
  • Google Contacts: names, email addresses, phone numbers and organisations.
  • Google Calendar: event times, titles and attendees.
  • Your Google account: your email address, to show which account is connected.

Orbit never sends, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.

How Orbit uses it

On your device, Orbit uses this data to build and keep up to date your private contacts list: who you know and when you were last in touch. Gmail headers also let Orbit notice when a copy of your data that you requested from another service is ready, so Orbit can remind you to import it.

Storage and sharing

  • Orbit stores your data in an encrypted database on your device, and your Google sign-in in your device’s Keychain. The sign-in is used only with Google.
  • If you use several devices, Orbit syncs your data and your Google sign-in between them, encrypted on your device first, directly or through your own private iCloud storage. We have no server that receives your data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data, and never use it for advertising.

Cloud AI

Orbit can answer questions about your contacts. When Cloud AI is on, Orbit may send excerpts derived from your connected sources, including Gmail, to our AI provider to answer your question; names, email addresses and phone numbers are replaced with placeholders first. The provider may not use them to train models. You can turn Cloud AI off in Orbit’s Settings, and then nothing is sent.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Orbit’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No person reads your Google data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

Orbit keeps your data until you delete it. Disconnecting a Google account in Orbit stops all reading and removes its sign-in from your devices. Deleting people in Orbit, or deleting Orbit from your devices, deletes your data; we hold no copy. You can also revoke Orbit’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
